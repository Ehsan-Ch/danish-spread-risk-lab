A transparent research setup

  1. Official data. 70,080 Energinet quarter-hour prices across DK1 and DK2, with UTC keys and Danish local delivery days.
  2. Time-aware inputs. Price lags start at 72 hours. Every label is assumed available only 24 hours after its interval ends.
  3. Separate calibration. 120 fit days, then 28 calibration days. The issue time is 10:00 local on D−1.
  4. Limits are visible. Revised historical data, familiar benchmark, no weather or network forecasts, and imperfect interval coverage.
